# Code of Conduct

BEYOND is built for everyone, and so is the community around it. We want taking part here (in issues, pull
requests, discussions and reviews) to be friendly, welcoming and free of harassment for everyone, whatever their
age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, nationality,
appearance, race, religion or sexual orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Assume good intentions, and ask before assuming.
- Give and take feedback gracefully: comment on the work, not the person.
- Own your mistakes, apologise to the people they affected, and learn from them.
- Think about what's best for the project and its users, not only yourself.

## What isn't acceptable

- Insults, put-downs, trolling, or personal or political attacks
- Harassment, in public or in private, including unwanted attention or contact
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Publishing someone's private information (such as their address or email) without permission
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Where this applies

Everywhere the project is discussed or worked on: this repository and its issues, pull requests and discussions,
and anywhere someone represents the project.

## Reporting a problem

If someone's behaviour worries you, contact the maintainer privately through
[GitHub](https://github.com/jeremystevens). Every report will be read and handled promptly and fairly, and the
reporter's privacy will be respected.

## What can happen

Depending on what happened, the maintainer may give a private warning, ask for an apology, remove comments or
contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban someone from the project.
